About this prototype

The problem

Every diner I interviewed trusted reviews on other platforms more than Beli's, so they left the app to double-check before deciding.

What this prototype does

  • Verify Your Visit: a Google Calendar match or a receipt upload. Always optional.
  • Proof of Visit replaces the unused Add Labels field, and never asks twice.
  • A skippable nudge when a review has no photo or note.
  • Progress toward a Verified Reviewer badge at 15 verified reviews.

What it leaves out

Real calendar access and receipt reading (both simulated), AI summaries, reputation sorting, share cards and creator programs, which are later phases in the PRD.
